# Glossary
- Run: one expedition from the foothills to the Beacon Keeper.
- Card: one persistent deck entry, copied into a combat's draw pile.
- Intent: the exact action an enemy will execute this turn.
- Block: temporary protection consumed by damage and cleared at the owner's next turn.
- Exhaust: a combat-only pile that cannot be reshuffled.
- Ember: a persistent modifier collected during a run.
- Route: one legal sequence of adjacent map nodes.
