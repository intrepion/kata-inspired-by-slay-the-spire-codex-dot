# Lantern Ascent specification
User-authorized defaults resolve the Grill design frontier without optional questions.
Original single-character, single-act, fixed-camera deckbuilding browser game inspired by the original Slay the Spire (2019, standard non-Ascension rules, not the sequel). Six route tiers, branching adjacent lanes, combat, event, elite, rest and boss. Seeded shuffle/reward selection, 3 energy, 5-card draw, discard reshuffle, exhaust, attack targeting, block clear at next owner turn, visible deterministic intent. Weak is 25% reduced attack rounded down; enemy debuffs tick after that enemy acts. Combat-local strength. Rest heals or upgrades; event can remove a card or grant an ember. Rewards offer three cards plus skip. Starter ember heals 4 after nonfatal victory. Elite grants first-turn +1 energy. Boss win and death are terminal. Versioned, namespaced local save; restart never modifies any other storage key. No accounts, backend, telemetry or external assets.
Test seams: public engine commands, serialization validation, and ordinary browser controls. Tests authorized in delegation. Seven-to-twelve minute target, tuning evidence recorded honestly. Original SVG/CSS artwork, no audio.
